I have worked on international climate politics, and in particular the negotiations under the UN Climate Change convention (UNFCCC) on and off for almost 20 years. I have been especially interested in 
- changes in the international climate policy regime and the development of the **Paris Agreement**
- the distributional conflict between **North and South** in international climate politics
- the role of forests in climate mitigation, and in particular the **REDD+** initiative

## Political work

From 2008 to 2013, I followed the UNFCCC negotiations very closely, participating in most negotiation sessions either as part of Norway's official delegation or as an NGO observer. At the time, I was working for Friends of the Earth Norway and Rainforest Foundation Norway, focusing in particular on the negotiations on REDD+ and Norway's International Climate and Forests Initiative.

Following my work on the UNFCCC negotiations as a practitioner, I have written extensively on international climate politics. In 2013, I published the Norwegian-language monograph [_Klimaspillet_](/en/pub/klimaspillet/), which details the negotiations around the failed Copenhagen summit in 2009 and explains the fundamental North/South conflict that has structured UN negotiations on climate change.

## Teaching and talks

I frequently give lectures on justice and the North/South dimension in international climate politics. I have also given a number of talks and interviews on the Paris Agreement, Norways role in international climate policy, and similar topics. However, I no longer follow the UNFCCC negotiations closely, except as it relates to questions about [fossil fuels](/en/div/energy).
